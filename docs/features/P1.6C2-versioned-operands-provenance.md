## Required input

Consume only a C1-verified, immutable evidence bundle bound to the exact machine, aggregation processor/stream/revision, period/context, schedule receipt, activity membership, classification/continuity versions and V2 policy.
C1-R1 (durable output-to-cut membership, including non-running without metric facts), C1-R2 (positive approved empty-schedule evidence) and C1-R3 (independent assessment/reference verification) remain required implementation deliverables. No test fixture or construction helper can bypass them.

Let S be the disjoint assessed UTC scope; P the C1-verified planned interval union; A the established activity partition over P; R the established Running fragments in S.
P comes from historical schedule authority, not from the extent of observed running or activity-derived planned facts. Referenced planned-duration facts must agree with that authority; disagreements are conflicts, not alternative denominators.

## Operand contract

| Operand | Construction | Admission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PlannedOperatingTime | Sum of lengths of disjoint P, in exact UTC ticks / ticks-per-second | Complete historical schedule/exclusion authority for S |
| EligibleRunningDuration | Sum of lengths of Running intersections with P | Complete authoritative activity and eligibility over all P |
| TotalRunningDuration | Sum of lengths of all disjoint Running fragments in S | Separate complete activity authority over S |
| ExcludedRunningDuration (diagnostic) | Sum of established Running intersections with S minus P | Value explicitly describes established fragments; complete-scope claim requires full-S proof |

A missing total-running operand must not block Availability when planned activity is complete. Diagnostic partial excluded running must carry PartialEstablishedFragments extent and never appear as a complete total.
Legacy ActualProductionTime keeps its current meaning. No global key remap and no changes to Performance/Utilization.
C3 owns whether and how operand outcomes become public metric statuses; C2 never converts missing evidence into numeric zero.

## Deterministic interval construction

1. Validate exact subject, policy and cut agreement for every receipt. Reject corrupt/foreign references; C1 insufficient/conflicting authority does not become a successful numeric operand bundle.
2. Validate positive half-open UTC endpoints, exact duration consistency, unique evidence identities and source membership before normalization. Use checked integer tick arithmetic; reject overflow without truncation.
3. Order semantic activity fragments by start ticks, end ticks and exact source identity. Reject temporal overlap, duplicate source fragments and incompatible classifications. Repeated references to the same interval from different metric keys are one source activity reference, not independent duration; validate their payload agreement before projection.
4. Build a boundary partition from S, P, activity and eligibility boundaries. Resolve every retained fragment to its authoritative source interval, schedule assignment/override and inclusion or exclusion receipt.
5. Intersect each Running fragment with P to produce eligible fragments. Intersect it with the established complement of P inside S to produce excluded fragments. Clip only at authoritative boundaries; never infer a break/cutoff from aggregate first/last timestamps.
6. Prove coverage of P by established classified activity using set equality, including non-running fragments. A temporal gap is never non-running. Adjacent spans are permitted; equal duration with different covered intervals is insufficient.
7. Sum admitted durations in ticks, convert to decimal seconds once, and retain the exact tick totals as provenance. Do not round operands or use floating-point TotalSeconds. C3 owns final display/precision policy.
8. Construct canonical provenance independently of input enumeration. Derive identities only after validation and deterministic ordering.

Do not subtract aggregate outside-plan running from total running to infer eligible running. Set intersections with retained provenance are the authoritative construction.

## Conservation and integrity

For every source Running fragment admitted to the complete reconstruction:
- Eligible and excluded fragments are disjoint and their union equals that fragment clipped to S.
- Their tick lengths sum exactly to the clipped source length.
- Eligible fragments are subsets of P; excluded fragments are subsets of S minus P.
- All fragments retain the original source interval identity plus clipped endpoints; splitting does not mint raw observations.

Across the scope, EligibleRunningDuration <= PlannedOperatingTime. When full-S running authority exists, TotalRunningDuration equals eligible plus excluded running. Without full-S authority, do not assert this equality for complete totals.
Negative duration, source overlap, contradictory eligibility, conservation failure or eligible > planned produces an explicit invariant failure for C3 or an integrity rejection; never clamp or silently deduplicate.
Proof of absence depends on C1 authority, not on the absence of Running inputs.

## Provenance manifest (logical schema)

Every operand binds:
- Exact V2 semantic identity and evidence policy/version.
- Full evaluation subject and aggregation cut; keep observation positions in their separate domain.
- C1 bundle/receipt identity, exact coverage subject AND revision where used, schedule snapshot/override/expansion references and completeness extent.
- Exact source output/fact identities, positions and historical membership references; a source without a metric fact uses its independently bound producer-output receipt.
- Original and clipped UTC tick endpoints, state/classification version, eligibility decision and its authority reference.
- Operand-specific ordered fragments, exact duration ticks, unit and operand admission/completeness result.
- Diagnostic excluded fragments and explicit extent, separate from admitted numeric operands.

For a zero numerator, retain the classified non-running coverage manifest and authoritative P; an empty eligible list alone cannot prove zero.
For a zero denominator, retain the positive no-production schedule receipt. TotalRunningDuration stays absent if activity is unknown; eligible intersection with proven empty P may be structurally zero but does not imply activity completeness or numeric Availability.

Canonical ordering: exact subject first; operand key ordinal; fragments by UTC start, UTC end, source-kind ordinal, source identity ordinal and source revision/position in its typed domain. Lists of references sort by kind/identity/revision using ordinal, culture-independent comparison. Preserve source spelling; no case folding or locale-sensitive normalization.
Do not merge adjacent provenance fragments across different authorities or identities. Interval unions used for coverage may normalize adjacency without discarding the fragment manifest.
C4 must select the canonical binary encoding, domain-separated content identity, null/empty representation and digest collision handling. C2 freezes canonical content/order, not an unreviewed wire format or hash algorithm.

## Boundary decisions and design examples

Times below are synthetic minutes within S=[0,60); exact tick arithmetic is required in implementation.

| Evidence | Expected construction |
| --- | --- |
| P=[0,60), Running=[0,30), established non-running=[30,60) | Eligible=30 min; planned=60 min |
| P=[0,30), Running=[0,60) with authoritative split | Eligible=30 min; excluded=30 min; total=60 min if full-S proof |
| P=[0,20) union [40,60), Running=[0,60) | Eligible=40 min; break running excluded=20 min |
| P=[0,60), non-running=[0,20) union [40,60), gap=[20,40) | No numeric eligible operand; middle-gap authority failure |
| P=[0,30), non-running=[0,30), activity unknown=[30,60) | Eligible=0 with planned proof; total absent |
| P proven empty, activity unknown | NoPlannedProduction authority path; total absent |
| P unknown, no Running inputs | InsufficientAuthority, never 0% |
| Same fragments supplied in different orders | Identical canonical provenance and totals |
| Identical tick totals but different source fragment identities | Different provenance; never substitute evidence |
| Running crosses cutoff but historical split authority missing | InsufficientAuthority; no guessed intersection |
| Overlapping source fragments or cross-cut payload | Conflict/integrity rejection before sums |
| Partial-day evidence against full-day P | No full-period numeric operand; as-of policy remains separately versioned |
