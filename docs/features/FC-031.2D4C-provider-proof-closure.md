## 3. Provider proof closed and frozen

The following FC-031.2D.4C provider proof obligations are now implemented, verified, and frozen.

### C-01 — Fresh committed publication with lost acknowledgement

```text
fresh NewPublication commits
caller does not observe successful acknowledgement
same retained proposal is resubmitted
    → Accepted / ExactReplay
```

The replay leaves the complete current authority tuple and revision unchanged and does not duplicate durable state/activity history.

### C-02 — Forward committed publication with lost acknowledgement

```text
established lineage
forward NewPublication commits
caller does not observe successful acknowledgement
same retained proposal is resubmitted
    → Accepted / ExactReplay
```

The replay preserves the already-committed forward projection, cumulative histories, EvaluationAuthority, and revision.

### C-03 — Repeated retained-proposal replay

Repeated direct resubmission of the same retained proposal remains `Accepted / ExactReplay` with stable:

```text
P = MachineStateActivityProjection
S = durable state-change history
A = durable activity-period history
E = EvaluationAuthority
ProjectionRevision
```

No replay allocates a revision or duplicates history.

### C-04 — Lost acknowledgement at `ulong.MaxValue`

The proof advances an established authority from `ulong.MaxValue - 1` to `ulong.MaxValue`, suppresses acknowledgement after the committed `NewPublication`, and then directly resubmits the retained proposal.

The retained proposal is classified as `Accepted / ExactReplay` at `ProjectionRevision = ulong.MaxValue` without attempting another revision allocation.

This confirms the inherited rule that complete-payload exact replay precedes revision-exhaustion classification.

### C-05 — Changed payload is not ExactReplay

A changed complete payload does not qualify as `ExactReplay` merely because expected tokens identify an existing state.

Subsequent classification remains governed by the inherited publication algebra:

```text
intrinsic proposal-local validation
→ complete-payload ExactReplay comparison
→ dual CAS validation
→ current-state advancement validation
→ revision-exhaustion classification
→ atomic NewPublication
```

The proof intentionally does not reinterpret all changed payloads as conflicts. A contradictory or non-forward proposal may conflict; a separately valid forward changed proposal may be accepted as `NewPublication`.

### C-06 — Processor/stream lineage isolation

Lost-acknowledgement recovery for one `(StateProcessorId, ObservationStreamId)` lineage does not mutate other processor/stream lineages. Their projection, durable histories, EvaluationAuthority, and revision remain unchanged.

## 4. Frozen proof mechanism boundary

The proof exercises only the semantic cut already frozen by the 4C contract inventory:

```text
joint provider commit has become authoritative
    ↓
caller has not observed successful acknowledgement
```

The test-only acknowledgement wrapper delegates publication to the real in-memory joint provider, allows the inner `NewPublication` to commit, then suppresses the successful acknowledgement by throwing a test-only exception.

The proof does not add a portable provider contract, provider-local post-commit fault mechanism, automatic retry policy, retry scheduler, retry owner, or processor retry behavior.

The retained proposal is resubmitted explicitly by the test. Therefore the proof remains provider-level evidence only.

## 5. Frozen complete replay oracle

For accepted retained-proposal recovery, the proof verifies the complete authoritative result:

```text
P unchanged
S history unchanged
A history unchanged
E unchanged
ProjectionRevision unchanged
```

Expected projection position and expected authority revision remain excluded from replay identity. Provider-assigned revision remains excluded from the submitted replay identity while the current committed revision itself must remain unchanged across exact replay.
