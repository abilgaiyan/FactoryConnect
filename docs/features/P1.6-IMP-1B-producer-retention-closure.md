## Concrete prospective closure relation (IMP-1A-R1)

Propose two immutable logical records, to be reviewed before any schema/API:
1. ProducerPublicationManifest: producer/observation stream; expected/next producer checkpoint; ordered activity/eligibility outputs with payload identities; source history revision/ordinal references; exact metric fact IDs and assigned metric-input positions; historical schedule selection receipt; policy versions; complete output/dependency inventory.
2. AggregationAuthorityAdmission: exact aggregation processor/stream/revision; referenced producer-manifest identities; explicitly admitted output IDs; associated fact-membership inventory; dependency closure and scope; completion/continuity/schedule receipt identities.

The producer must capture actual positions returned by the metric-input writer inside the same transaction; IDs/position allocation cannot be guessed later. Its complete manifest co-commits with outputs/facts/checkpoint.
The aggregation revision must co-commit its admission record under the same owner as that exact revision. A separately attached later record cannot retrospectively complete the earlier revision.

Admission algorithm:
- Read incorporated fact set F for exact cut C from the contribution ledger, not from latest facts.
- For each candidate output O, resolve its producer manifest and required fact membership D(O). Include O as activity/eligibility authority only when every required fact member is in F and all source/schedule dependencies verify.
- If D(O) is empty (output-only evidence), admission requires an explicit output-only entry co-committed with C, binding the producer manifest, source authority cut and exact output subset. Empty dependencies do not grant implicit admission.
- An admitted manifest is not wholesale admission of its other outputs. Cross-output dependencies close recursively; incomplete closure yields Unproven for the affected extent.
- Validate required fact IDs/positions/keys/payloads, not merely position<=C. Foreign stream or duplicate/conflicting payload rejects.
- Non-admitted outputs can remain diagnostics only if clearly labeled; they cannot fill planned coverage or extend the numerator.

Concrete partial-batch example: one producer manifest emits O1 with required facts at 10/11 and O2 at 12/13. An actual committed aggregation revision at 11 may admit O1 after verification, not O2. A revision at 10 cannot admit full O1 under this conservative rule. Output-only O3 is excluded unless revision 11 explicitly admits its dependency-closed authority. These numbers are synthetic metric-input positions, never observation positions.
Conservative full-output membership may withhold otherwise derivable evidence at a partial cut; this is intentional pending review. Do not claim this admission algorithm is implemented or was historically recorded.

## State evidence without running facts (IMP-1A-R2)

Scheduled-duration facts are source-link candidates for all states, including Unknown. Resolve the underlying contextualized activity state, source history and continuity authority before admission.
No Running fact does not imply stopped/non-running. Unknown, Offline and missing signal quality require the bound classification/continuity policy to establish their treatment; do not infer eligible zero.
A still-open active state cannot be closed at reporting end by wall-clock time alone. Require retained source-backed completion evidence or leave the uncovered planned suffix Unproven.
Verify deployed activity composition separately; the inspected continuity calculator alone does not prove production behavior.

## Historical schedule provenance (IMP-1A-R3)

Required historical selection payload: effective assignments, planned/break windows, overrides, timezone and expansion version, resolved UTC scope, positive complete selection/approved-empty decision and derivation binding.
In-memory configured assignments and POT-1 IDs do not contain this whole receipt. Roster rows identify shift occurrences, not necessarily the complete planned production selection.
Inspect any retained deployment/config archives only after authorization and bind exact version/effective scope to the historical producer. Never treat matching window timestamps as provenance.
If only current configuration exists, return MissingSchedule authority for historical V2; do not rewrite old facts or fabricate an old receipt.
