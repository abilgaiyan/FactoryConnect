## Closed provider chain

FC-031.2E is closed across the following frozen slices:

- **2E.A — Provider-Owned Authority Instance Graph**: the InMemory provider owns one compatible acquisition, mapping, state/activity, and authority-cut instance graph.
- **2E.B — In-Memory Stable Authority-Cut Provider**: bounded optimistic stable-cut validation, maximum three attempts, no delay/backoff, coherent binding and lineage validation, stable null/partial/complete cuts, and unavailable classification for instability or impossible/incompatible lineage.
- **2E.C — Capability Activation**: `CurrentStateAuthorityReading` explicitly activates the provider-owned `ICurrentStateAuthorityCutProvider`; providers that do not supply the capability remain outside this authority surface.
- **2E.D — Observation Handoff**: one Edge observation authority-store graph selects the mapping/state ownership pair, and all compatible authority interfaces and observation consumers derive from that selected pair.
- **2E.E — Production Handoff**: one production activity association derives from the 2E.D-selected state/activity store when observation participates, owns one compatible InMemory fallback when production is standalone, and supplies the authoritative `IProductionContextActivityReader` without registration-order ownership ambiguity.
- **2E.F — Whole-Provider Closure**: one finalized InMemory composition proves the provider-owned authority graph across both the public stable-cut surface and the public production activity-history surface.

## Frozen whole-provider invariant

Within one finalized InMemory Edge service-provider graph, acquisition/contact authority, mapping coverage authority, state/activity authority, stable current-state authority-cut reading, and production activity-history reading must remain projections of one coherent provider-owned authority graph.

When observation processing participates, production activity history must derive from the state/activity store selected by the observation authority graph. The public `ICurrentStateAuthorityCutProvider` must observe the same provider-owned acquisition, mapping, and evaluation lineage. Independent replacement, duplicate ownership selection, or registration-order displacement is not permitted.
