# P1.6C1 — Historical eligibility and activity completeness authority

Status: proposed implementation-design contract for review. Documentation only.
Baseline: `0dfc439714cbfec512a77c3a3285d6bf25977435` (PR #129).
Semantic authority: [accepted P1.6A/B](P1.6-planned-production-availability-v2.md). Do not reopen legacy operand semantics.
C1-C4 below are P1.6 stages, not the earlier B1 concurrency case identifiers.

## Decision and limits

Numeric V2 Availability requires independently verifiable historical schedule/eligibility authority and complete activity classification over the planned interval union at one exact aggregation cut.
A persisted flag, aggregate extent, advanced checkpoint or structurally Complete coverage assessment alone cannot establish this.
Proven zero planned time needs complete schedule authority and contradiction checks, but not activity completeness (R2).
This proposal introduces no runtime interfaces, schema, evaluator, replay or deployment.

## Proposed evidence bundle (logical shape, not a wire format)

Reserve evidence policy `planned-production-availability-evidence/v2`. A verified bundle binds:

1. **Subject:** V2 projection processor and definition/policy, machine, company/site/line, full period identity, context partition, exact aggregation processor/stream/position.
2. **Schedule authority receipt:** immutable content identity and version; assignment and override content; effective dates and roster selection; planned/break exclusion rules; timezone plus expansion rule/version and resolved UTC boundaries; explicit assessed scope and complete selection result. An established empty selection must distinguish an approved no-production decision from absent configuration.
3. **Source membership receipt:** incorporated fact IDs/positions and source eligibility/contextualized activity output IDs with payload identity; exact producer/stream and historical publication membership linking each output to the selected aggregation cut. Include the complete relevant output inventory, not only running facts.
4. **Activity authority receipt:** exact classified fragments and source observation references; classification and continuity policy versions; durable boundary/completion evidence; unresolved spans and exclusions. Define which evidence establishes Running/non-running; Unknown or Offline is not implicitly established non-running.
5. **Reconciliation receipt:** assessed interval union S, planned union P, authoritative classified activity union A, eligible running union E, missing activity P minus A, unknown eligibility and conflicts; exact referenced coverage subject AND coverage revision if the existing coverage store is used.
6. **Verification identity:** canonical evidence content/encoding version and digest, verifier policy/version, exact source cuts and authority references. A digest verifies content identity, not authority by itself.

These receipts are proposed requirements, not claims that these records or producers already exist.
C4 must settle codecs, hashes, durable references, retention/FKs and transaction ownership before implementation. C1 forbids an unverified caller Boolean such as complete=true.

## Cut coherence and historical selection

Aggregation input positions and observation positions are different domains: never compare them numerically or infer one from the other.
Resolve each incorporated fact through its exact eligibility output, contextualized activity output and producer publication to its own observation evidence. Establish a historical membership map or immutable producer receipt; do not read the latest production-context checkpoint as a proxy.
A referenced producer output may have a different position domain, but must be proven incorporated/available for this exact cut. New producer activity not incorporated in the aggregation revision cannot fill its gaps.
For boundaries or non-running evidence with no metric fact, require an explicit historical publication binding to that cut; without it, completeness is Unproven.

The schedule receipt must be the immutable schedule selection actually bound to the historical derivation. A current configuration, even with the same assignment ID, is not acceptable.
Do not attach today's schedule to old facts merely because interval endpoints match. Missing historical schedule evidence stays missing. Any later recovered authority requires separate admission, provenance and versioning review; no silent upgrade of a prior exact result.

All independent reconstruction inputs must be read under a caller-owned stable transaction or from immutable, verified historical receipts with pinned identities. C4 owns the final SQL lock order and atomic verification/publication mechanism.
A receipt read before publication must be independently revalidated during publication; detached preflight success grants no write authority.

## Verification algorithm and outcomes

1. Validate subject/source cut existence and ownership. Corrupt or contradictory retained identities/receipts reject historical read/publication.
2. Resolve exact schedule selection, exclusions and timezone expansion for S. Missing selection/extent produces InsufficientEvidence; empty database query results alone cannot prove no production.
3. Validate all submitted relevant contributions and receipts for identity, positive UTC tick duration, membership, duplicate/overlap and eligibility contradictions. Reject structural/durable corruption; report established semantic conflicts without a number. Do not use known contradictory evidence merely because it is outside P.
4. Build P as a disjoint union clipped to S from schedule authority. Verify eligibility fragments against P and its complement. Explicit false requires a valid exclusion receipt; missing/null eligibility never becomes false.
5. If P is proven empty after validation, produce the authority decision NoPlannedProduction with no numeric ratio. Machine activity may remain unknown; do not publish a fabricated total-running operand. Missing activity is distinct from contradictory evidence.
6. For nonempty P, resolve all activity intersecting P, split at authoritative boundaries and verify continuity/completion. Compute G = P minus A. Nonempty G or unresolved classification/eligibility in P blocks every numeric result, including zero.
7. Only if G is empty and all required authority is verified, expose the verified planned/activity interval evidence to C2. C2 constructs E from established Running intersections; C3 applies the formula. C1 does not compute Availability.

Proposed authority outcomes: VerifiedPlannedActivity, VerifiedNoPlannedProduction, InsufficientAuthority, ConflictingAuthority; historical integrity failures throw/reject rather than become success.
C3 must map these to the frozen status/reason precedence. No new enum is implemented here.

## Coverage reconciliation and boundaries

S is the exact disjoint assessed scope; no bridging shift/day gaps. P is independent of observed activity and may not be reconstructed only from activity-derived planned-duration facts.
A contains established classified activity fragments within P. Its union must equal P to authorize numeric Availability; no duration-only equality shortcut.
Unknown eligibility, gaps, and classified spans must form an explicit disjoint reconciliation. Missing intervals are derived by set subtraction, not by InputCount or first/last timestamps.
Adjacency is permitted; overlaps/duplicate semantic source fragments are rejected before union normalization. Distinct state-duration keys describing one interval are not separate activity fragments.
State change boundaries and period-end closure require source-backed completion evidence. Wall-clock time, an HTTP heartbeat or ingestion continuing elsewhere cannot close an activity interval.
Counter resets, source-instance changes, mapping changes and acquisition gaps must be resolved under the bound continuity policy; an unexplained boundary leaves its affected span unproven.
Missing activity outside P does not invalidate Availability; TotalRunningDuration requires a separate full-S completeness decision. Do not silently present a partial total as complete.
A complete historic schedule with zero planned time remains distinguishable from missing activity and missing schedule authority.

## Existing coverage contract reuse

Reuse the existing subject/revision selection and structural interval validation where compatible. For numeric Availability, expected coverage is P and classified coverage is A, with a verifier-established completion boundary and exact schedule/evidence references.
A stored Complete assessment is only an input receipt: independently resolve its references and reconcile at the bound cut before admitting it.
Null expected intervals remain unknown; empty expected intervals require positive historical schedule authority. Do not reinterpret every V1 Complete assessment as V2 evidence.
For zero planned time, use schedule authority directly; do not manufacture activity evidence merely to satisfy the V1 assessment constructor.
C4 must decide whether a new proof envelope can reference an existing assessment unchanged or needs a versioned extension. No schema reuse assumption is frozen by C1.

## Required acceptance cases (not executed)

| Case | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Non-running covers P exactly, no running facts | VerifiedPlannedActivity; C2 eligible zero permitted |
| Middle gap within P with non-running either side | InsufficientAuthority; no numeric zero |
| Activity missing only outside P | Planned proof admitted; complete total-running proof withheld |
| Complete no-production schedule and unknown activity | VerifiedNoPlannedProduction |
| Empty schedule read without completeness receipt | InsufficientAuthority |
| Break/cutoff crossed with authoritative boundaries | Deterministic split; false fragments require exclusion authority |
| Boolean disagrees with pinned schedule, overlap or duplicate activity | Conflict/rejection; no clamp or deduplication |
| Old aggregation revision plus newer activity/assessment | Reject cross-cut substitution |
| Correct opaque reference whose payload is absent or changed | Missing authority or integrity rejection, never label-based admission |
| Source-instance/mapping discontinuity unresolved | Affected planned span unproven |
| Same cut with reordered evidence | Identical canonical proof; conflicting receipt rejected |
| Current schedule changed after historic derivation | Historic pinned receipt unchanged; no current-config fallback |
| Completion boundary short of planned scope | No full-period number; separately versioned as-of scope required |

## Exit gate and follow-on

C1 freeze requires review of source membership binding, immutable schedule selection, completeness producer/receipt and positive no-production authority. These are required implementation deliverables, not optional TODOs.
If a historical receipt cannot be reconstructed from retained authority, the supported result is InsufficientAuthority; P1.6D must report that limitation rather than invent historical completeness.
C2 owns operand/provenance construction; C3 owns formula and result precedence; C4 owns publication formats, independent revalidation, replay and concurrency.
P1.6A/B remains closed. C1 design awaits review. All production implementation, GG-003 replay, SQL migration and deployment remain held. Factory schedule approval remains a separate business prerequisite.
